Figure 3. Genome structure and candidate hydrolysate-stress genes in Chlorella sp. HS2 and its laboratory-evolved derivative ALE. (A) Both strains were assembled de novo from PacBio HiFi long reads to near-complete BUSCO scores and are collinear at the whole-genome scale. Elevated BUSCO duplication rates, a size gap against an independent k-mer estimate, and specific self-alignment duplicate blocks together indicate real duplicated genomic content in both genomes — but a direct genotyping-based measurement of HS2 heterozygosity (0.0036%, ~1 heterozygous site per 27,500 bp) is 30–300× lower than a typical outcrossing diploid, ruling out the project's original working assumption of a conventional heterozygous diploid genome. The duplicated content instead reflects at least three distinct, coexisting mechanisms (embedded repeat-rich patches, a dispersed repeat family, and at least one clean large-scale duplicate pair), not a single explanation. (B) Two methodologically independent approaches — read-based joint genotyping against the HS2 reference, and direct whole-genome assembly alignment — converge on the same order of magnitude for ALE-specific small variants, a meaningful cross-check between unrelated methods. Structural-variant caller concordance is low (2 of 26 unique loci confirmed by both pbsv and Sniffles2), which is typical for SV calling in general and is reported here as a caveat on confidence rather than a pipeline error. (C) Three candidate genes for the hydrolysate-stress phenotype driving ALE's selection — PEL1/SLAD and ODP25/EPHA (missense, MODERATE impact) and SYGM2/GLOX (frameshift, HIGH impact) — all fall in the furan/aldehyde-detoxification category, consistent with the furanic compounds (e.g. 5-HMF) hypothesized to be a primary selective pressure in the hydrolysate medium. Unlike the HS6 findings elsewhere in this project, none of these three candidates have yet had the individual coverage/contig-edge sanity check applied that is standard practice in this project's pipeline (the same check that previously caught an ABC-transporter-tagged frameshift as a pure assembly artifact) — they are reported here as leads under active investigation, not confirmed causal variants. No transcriptome or expression dataset exists for this strain pair, so this figure summarizes genome-assembly and variant-calling evidence only; it is not a metabolic pathway map.
Assembly: hifiasm 0.25.0 (HiFi, diploid mode), organelle-read-depleted. Variant calling: DeepVariant + GLnexus joint genotyping (read-based), pbsv + Sniffles2 (structural variants), MUMmer4/dnadiff (whole-genome alignment). QC: density filter (±500 bp) then per-variant coverage/contig-edge filter. Heterozygosity: direct genotyping of HS2 reads against the HS2 assembly. Full analysis: Chlorella sp. HS2 × ALE comparative genomics reports (this site).
